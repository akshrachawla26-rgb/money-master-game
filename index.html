Money Master - Game Rules & Instructions

Objective

Navigate through financial challenges, make smart decisions, answer questions, identify risks, and reach the finish line with the highest **Financial Literacy Score (FLS)** and accumulated **Capital (₹)**.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Player Setup: Select 2 to 4 players. Each player begins at Space 1 (START) with an initial capital of ₹50,000 and 100 Literacy Points.
  2. Turn Mechanics: Players take turns rolling the 6-sided dice. The player token automatically advances by the number rolled.
  3. Landing on Spaces: When landing on a space, a challenge card triggers corresponding to that space category:
    • Chance Cards: Handles unexpected financial emergencies, investment windfalls, or market changes.
    • Decision Cards: Choose between two financial options (e.g., FD vs High-Yield Savings). Read options carefully!
    • Knowledge Cards: Multiple-choice questions on digital banking, UPI safety, compounding, and taxation.
    • Risk Cards: Identify phishing scams, fraudulent QR codes, and Ponzi schemes.
    • Calculation Cards: Solve practical budgeting, compound interest, or loan interest calculations.
    • Reward Spaces: Bonus points and capital cash rewards for financial discipline.
  4. Rewards & Penalties: Correct decisions and answers earn +₹5,000 to +₹15,000 and +20 Literacy Points. Incorrect choices or falling for scams incur cash losses and point deductions.
  5. Winning the Game: The game ends when all players reach Space 24 (FINISH) or complete the board. The player with the highest total financial score (Capital + [Literacy Points × ₹500]) is crowned the Money Master!

Financial Literacy Visual Infographics

1. The Power of Compounding

Starting early allows compound interest to exponential grow your wealth over time ($A = P(1 + r/n)^{nt}$).

2. The 50/30/20 Budgeting Rule

Allocate monthly income wisely: 50% Needs, 30% Wants, and 20% Savings/Investments.

3. Risk vs Return Spectrum

Savings Bank / FD Low Risk | Low Return (3-7%)
Mutual Funds / Debt Moderate Risk | Moderate Return (8-12%)
Direct Equities / Stocks High Risk | High Return (12-18%+)
Unregulated Ponzi Schemes Extreme Risk | FRAUD (Loss of Principal)

4. Digital Payment Security Rules

  • PIN is ONLY for deducting money: You NEVER enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
  • Verify QR Codes: Beware of callers sending QR codes claiming to send rewards or refunds.
  • Check SMS Alerts: Always read transaction confirmation messages from official bank sender IDs.
  • Grievance Redressal: Report unauthorized transactions to your bank immediately within 3 days.
